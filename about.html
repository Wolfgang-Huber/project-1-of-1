The idea

Project 1 of 1 is not a real company but a hobby. I, Wolfgang Huber, do lots of different projects for fun, mainly to learn and experiment.

All of my services and products are free to use or were given away for free. I never charge any commission, and a real business is not in my interest. It’s just a hobby of mine.

I hope you try some of my projects out. I made them with a lot of passion and energy. Enjoy!

Why Project 1 of 1?

There is only one of each kind.

I love making different projects, exploring and learning new things. Building the same project twice isn’t planned for now. I’d rather explore and do just one project of each thing. So every project is not only unique, it’s also one of one.
